About us

Many years ago I fell in love with the Maine Coon breed. I watched videos I found on YouTube and read all about the character of these cats. I was completely captivated by them. I have had alley cats around me all my life, and the Maine Coon was my dream. Then the moment came and my dream came true.

I took the Pawpeds course and am still educating myself on breeding. The welfare of cats is very important to me, and that is why I adapt everything to my cats. The cats simply live with us — watch TV with us, assist us with showering, preparing food and cleaning. We have common areas, a terrace and an aviary.

I have also become very interested in their diet, and thanks to them I have become a consultant for a company that produces pet food. We feed only quality food, which you can find linked below.
