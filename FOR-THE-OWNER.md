# Your website — how to change anything

Everything on your website can be changed by you, from one page.
No code, no help needed.

---

## Signing in

The first time, you'll get an **e-mail invitation**. Click the link, choose your
own password, and you're in.

After that, go to **simonscattery.be/admin** whenever you like.

---

## What you'll see

Three groups on the left:

**Cats** — every cat, kings and queens together
**Blog posts** — every article
**Pages** — Home, Kittens, Our area, Blog heading, Our cats covers, Contact

Click anything to open it.

---

## How to change something

1. Click it.
2. A form opens, already filled in.
3. Change what you want.
4. Press **Publish** at the top.

The website updates itself about half a minute later. Open it in another tab
and look.

---

## Titles and show results

Each cat has a switch: **This cat has titles or show results**.

Turn it on, and a gold section appears on her page where you can add:

- each **title** (Champion, Best Kitten…) with where and when
- **photos from the shows**

Leave the switch off and nothing shows — exactly as before.

---

## The rest, briefly

**Photos** — click a photo box, then either pick one already on the site or
drag a new one in from your phone or computer.

**Adding and removing** — litters, photo sections, titles and the little boxes
on the home page all have an **Add** button underneath and an **×** to remove.

**The order of things** — every cat and every post has a **Position** number.
Smaller number = higher up.

**Green italic words** — in a heading, put the word between `<em>` and `</em>`.
So `Our <em>Queens</em>` shows as *Our **Queens***.

**Writing a blog post** — put each paragraph between `<p>` and `</p>`, and each
heading between `<h2>` and `</h2>`.

**A cat with a page but no card** — turn off *Show on the Kings / Queens page*.
Her page stays, but she disappears from the list.

---

## Removing a cat or a post

Open it, then **Delete entry** at the top. Photos stay on the server, and every
previous version is kept, so nothing is ever really lost.

---

## Good to know

- Changes take about 30 seconds to appear. Refresh the page if you don't see it.
- Every change is saved forever — nothing can be lost.
- Photos straight from a phone are fine.
- If you change your mind before publishing, just leave the page.
